A Basic Guide to Basketball

A game played between two teams, typically of five players each, on a rectangular court. Each team tries to score by shooting, laying up, or dunking the ball through the opponent’s goal, an elevated horizontal hoop and net called a basket (Britannica, 2026).

Basic Rules
  • The ball may be thrown in any direction with one or both hands.
  • The ball may be batted in any direction with one or both hands (never with fist).
  • A player cannot run with the ball.
